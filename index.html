Pit Lane Sim Racing Club

A student-run club for anyone who likes racing games with a wheel and pedals. Beginners and fast drivers race together every week.

More ways to race with us

GT Sprint Nights

Short, close races in GT cars every week. Thirty minutes of racing, no pit stops, and a fresh track each night.

Details
Practice opens at 6:30 p.m. and qualifying runs for ten minutes before the race. Everyone uses the same fixed setup, so the result comes down to driving, not tuning.

GT Sprint Nights

Seats fill up fast. Send us a note on the Contact page and we will hold one of the 20 grid spots for you this week.

Gravel Rally Sundays

Point-to-point stages on loose surfaces. You race the clock, not each other, so it is a relaxed way to learn car control.

Details
Each Sunday has four stages. Your times are added together, and a co-driver from the club can read pace notes to you over voice chat if you want one.

Gravel Rally Sundays

No rally experience needed. Pick the Rally option on our Contact form and we will pair you with a co-driver for your first event.

6-Hour Endurance Cup

Teams of three share one car through day, dusk and night. Strategy, fuel and clean driver swaps decide who wins.

Details
Each driver must complete at least one stint of 90 minutes. Teams plan pit windows together on a shared sheet before the race starts.

6-Hour Endurance Cup

The next cup runs once a term. Tell us if you already have teammates, or we can place you on a team that needs a third driver.

Tandem Drift Jam

Not about lap times at all. Drivers pair up and follow each other sideways, scored on angle, line and how close they stay.

Details
Judges score the lead car on line and angle, and the chase car on how closely it copies the lead. Drivers then swap places and run again.

Tandem Drift Jam

Bring a wheel with a handbrake button if you have one. We run a short warm-up session so new drifters can practise solo first.

Open-Wheel Ladder

Climb from slower junior formula cars to fast single-seaters as your race results and safety rating improve.

Details
There are three tiers. You move up after finishing three races in a tier with no more than two incident points per race.

Open-Wheel Ladder

Everyone begins in the junior tier. Send us your preferred race night and we will add you to the next ladder round.

Rookie Setup Clinic

New to sim racing? Bring your wheel and pedals, and club members will help you set them up so the car feels right.

Details
We cover wheel rotation, force feedback strength, pedal calibration and seating position. Sessions are small, with one helper for every three rookies.

Rookie Setup Clinic

Clinics run on the first Saturday of each month. No equipment yet? You can try one of the club rigs during the session.
